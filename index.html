    How it works

    1. Connect or paste

      Connect Phantom, Solflare or Backpack. You can also paste any address to see what is locked there, but only the owner can close accounts.

    2. We scan for empty accounts

      NOTBOT reads your token accounts through Helius and keeps only those with a zero balance. Accounts that hold tokens are never touched.

    3. Review and sign

      You see the total, the fee and what you receive. Your wallet then asks you to approve the transaction. Nothing happens without your signature.

    Fees, in full

    Each token account keeps a rent deposit of about 0.00203928 SOL. Closing the account releases it. This is what happens to one account:

    Where it goesShareSOL per account
    Released by the network100%0.00203928
    NOTBOT service fee15%0.00030589
    Sent to your wallet85%0.00173339

    The fee is a separate transfer inside the same transaction you sign, so you can read it in your wallet's preview. You also pay the normal Solana network fee, a fraction of a cent.

    Safety

    Non-custodial

    NOTBOT is a static page. It builds a transaction in your browser and your wallet signs it. We never ask for a seed phrase or a private key, and we have no server that could receive one.

    Telegram trading bots

    Trading bots create many empty accounts. To reclaim from a bot wallet, export its key inside the bot, import it into Phantom, then open NOTBOT in Phantom's built-in browser. Once you have imported a bot key anywhere, treat that wallet as exposed and move your funds to a fresh wallet afterwards.

    Read before you sign

    A legitimate NOTBOT transaction only contains account-close instructions and one SOL transfer of 15% of the released rent to the fee wallet listed below. If you see anything else, reject it.

    Questions

    Will I lose any tokens?

    No. Only accounts with a balance of exactly zero are closed. Accounts holding tokens, or that are frozen, are skipped.

    Why do empty accounts exist at all?

    Solana requires a rent-exempt deposit to keep account data on chain. When you sell a token completely, the account stays open and the deposit stays locked until someone closes it.

    I have many accounts. Is it one transaction?

    Accounts are grouped into batches of 18 per transaction. You approve all batches at once when your wallet supports it, otherwise one after another.

    Do I need SOL in the wallet?

    Yes, a tiny amount to pay the network fee, around 0.00001 SOL per transaction.

    Can a closed account be recovered?

    Closing removes the empty account. If you buy that token again, a new account is created automatically.
